The first public version of Countdown Mini.

- Desktop widgets that count down the days to your events
- One widget in three sizes: small shows one event, medium two side by side, large three. With fewer events the large one gives each more room
- Choose each widget's events with a right-click → Edit Widget. Click a widget to open its first event in the app
- Count every day or only working days (Monday to Friday), with or without today
- A bar of the time left on a scale you choose: 7, 30 or 90 days, or a year
- In the last 24 hours before a timed event the widget becomes a ticking timer; then "Today", then "N days ago"
- A background for every event: 20 gradients, two colours of your own, or a picture from your Mac
- 70 icons, or any emoji
- No account, no network, no notifications. Your events are a file on your Mac

**Install:** download `Countdown-Mini.pkg` and open it. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Pictures of each step are on https://homeapplabs.com/install/

Then right-click the desktop → Edit Widgets and add Countdown Mini.

Works on macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
